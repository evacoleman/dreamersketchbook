About me:

I am currently studying Visual Communication Design, but I also studied Computer Science and Engineering for about 1½ years. I am hoping to add a minor in Video Game Studies before I graduate. I transferred to SFSU from Sacramento, CA, where I was born and raised. Outside of school, I have a lot of creative interests. I like video games, drawing, crocheting, watching anime, and playing several instruments. I love learning, so picking up new hobbies has always been an interest of mine.

With my background in Computer Science, I have some experience coding, so I am mainly hoping to learn how to make a website look nice in this class. I have really enjoyed being able to tie together all of the design principles I have learned into code. I decided to go into Graphic Design because even though I like coding, I felt that I missed being creative. Computer Science was too technical, and Studio Art was too intimidating, so Graphic Design became my Goldilocks.

I am looking forward to learning more in this class and getting to know everyone! If you would like to learn more about me, here is my instagram.
