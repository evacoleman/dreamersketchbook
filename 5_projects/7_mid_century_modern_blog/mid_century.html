About My Process

I had originally started the assignment too early and felt myself feeling some frusteration at what I did not know how to do. After the lecture, a lot of it was cleared up for me. However, even revisitng my code with fresh eyes, I was still running into issues. Whether it was the code not behaving how I thought it was or simply not knowing how to do something. Instead of getting tunnel vision on one issue I jumped between different parts I was working on. This helped me keep my sanity and allowed me to try out different techniques. Even though I was not able to get me website to look exactly like the example, I am proud of how much I accomplished. I feel that this assignment was a bigger step up from our previous ones, so I am excited to keep learning and improving my skills. With less knowledge of the code it is harder to troubleshoot, so I am hoping as I learn more I will also be able to address the problems I am having now.
