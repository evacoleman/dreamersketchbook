This homework assignment was really fun to do. Being able to experiment with how the stars moved gave me ideas of how to make my own website more interactive. It was interesting to see how changing the order of the transformations produced different results. For my first star I decided to translate and rotate it to give the effect of a shooting star. My second star rotates and my third star scales bigger. My fourth star scales smaller, while spinning. For the stars with rotation transformations I added a duration so it would noticeably spin instead of locking into a new rotation.
